Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner to copy the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data from any other person.
  • Google Drive access is used only to read and write the owner's backup files.
  • No data is sent to any third party.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
